About the Event

Where night
meets nature

Step into an enchanted world where cherry blossoms glow under the night sky at David Lam Park. Blossoms After Dark transforms Vancouver's most beloved spring landmark into an immersive evening experience.


Wander through illuminated canopies, discover surprise performers around every bend, feast from a curated lineup of food trucks, and create lasting memories beneath a sky of pink petals.

"You never know what surprise will lay around each corner."

Getting There

Find your way

Parking is limited — we encourage sustainable transport options.

🚆
SkyTrain
Exit Yaletown–Roundhouse Station. Head southeast on Davie St, turn left on Pacific Blvd — approximately 4–7 minutes on foot.
Translink schedules →
🚌
Bus
Several routes service the Yaletown and False Creek area with stops along Pacific Blvd. Short walk to David Lam Park from there.
Translink routes →
⛴️
False Creek Ferry
Granville Island's original little blue ferries — fast, frequent, and friendly service right to David Lam Park all weekend.
